Things you might have thought before…

  • Linguists are people who speak lots of languages?
    • But now you know that we’re just people who study language systematically, whether we speak certain ones or not
    • Linguistics = scientific study of language
  • English has five vowels – a, e, i, o, and u (and sometimes y)?
    • But now you know English has a lot more than that!
    • IPA = one-to-one correspondence!
  • Parrots talk, just like humans?
    • But now you know that things like arbitrariness, creativity, permitting displacement, etc. are hallmarks of a language, and most animal communication systems lack at least one of these elements!
    • A mode ≠ a system
  • Dialects are bad; everyone should speak the standard?
    • But now you know that all language varieties are systematic and serve the needs of their speakers
    • And you know that no one speaks the same way all the time
    • AND you know that if dialects didn’t accomplish something for speakers, people would stop using them
    • AND you know that the standard is hard to define
    • Dialects are languages!
