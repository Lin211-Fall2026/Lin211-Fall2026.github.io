  • Linguistics is the study of language
    • ≠ learning many languages
    • ≠ studying a language with the goal of learning to communicate in it
    • ≠ being the grammar police
  • Scientific, systematic exploration of how language works
    • Includes the study of the structure of language (how do we make a sentence?) and the use of language (how do we speak with one another?)
    • Linguists are interested in descriptive grammar , not focused on prescriptive grammar ::: {.notes}
